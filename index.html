Our Story

Woodo began in a small Copenhagen workshop with a simple belief: that the tools of creativity should be as beautiful as the art they help create.

Today, we curate the finest wooden art supplies from artisans across Scandinavia and beyond. Every shelf, every canvas, every brush is chosen to inspire.

We believe in slow commerce — thoughtful purchases that last a lifetime, not a season.
